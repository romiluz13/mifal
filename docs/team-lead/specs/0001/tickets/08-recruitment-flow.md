# 08: Recruitment flow — capability gap to pitched specialist

**What to build:** The lead notices the fleet lacks a capability (mission vs skill
inventory vs tool ledger), searches the skill pool, and pitches a new specialist to the
human: who, why, what it enables, cost — with the lead's recommendation. The human
approves or declines; hiring is human-reserved, always. On approval, the new member
onboards through its role folder.

**Blocked by:** 02 (Stewardship registries), 05 (Role folders).

**Status:** done

- [x] A planted capability gap against a demo mission triggers detection (no false
      pitch when the fleet covers the mission)
- [x] The pitch is a durable artifact to the human with who/why/what-it-enables/cost
      and a recommendation
- [x] No hire happens without explicit human approval (demo: approval gate holds)
- [x] After approval, onboarding runs through the role folder and the inventory gains
      the new member's capability brief
- [x] A decline is recorded with its reason and the gap stays visible in the record
